about us

♡ ❀ ♡

Bae's Chinchillas is a small breeder located just outside Kungälv in Västra Götaland, Sweden.

Our goal with both breeding and showing is to raise healthy, friendly chinchillas with excellent quality and temperament. We are currently focusing primarily on Royal Persian Angora (RPA) and Ebony mutations.

Alongside our breeding, we also have rescue chinchillas who are looking for loving, forever homes. Every chinchilla deserves a safe and caring environment where they can thrive, and we are happy to help them find the right home.

If you are interested in welcoming a chinchilla into your home, we encourage you to explore the information throughout our website to learn more about their needs and determine whether a chinchilla is the right pet for you.
