Why Donate?

♡ ❀ ♡

We at Bae's Chinchillas try our best to take in any and all chinchillas that are in need of homes.

Every rescue that comes into our care is provided with a safe environment, proper nutrition, enrichment and everything else they need while we work towards finding them the right forever home. Some chinchillas may only stay with us for a short period of time, while others may remain in our care for much longer.

Taking in rescue chinchillas comes with additional expenses, and donations help us provide the food, supplies, housing and care they need throughout their time with us. Your support helps us continue giving chinchillas in need a safe place to stay while they wait for their forever homes.

Donations are never expected, but every contribution, no matter the size, is greatly appreciated and makes a meaningful difference to the chinchillas and rescues in our care.

How Does It Help?

♡ ❀ ♡

Donations help us cover the everyday supplies, equipment and unexpected expenses involved in caring for our chinchillas and rescues.

♡ Chinchilla Food

High-quality chinchilla pellets for our own chinchillas and rescues.

♡ Timothy Hay & Alfalfa Cubes

Fresh hay and suitable forage products are an essential part of daily chinchilla care.

♡ KD Pine Wood Shavings

Kiln-dried pine shavings are used as suitable bedding in our cages.

♡ Anti-Pill Fleece

Anti-pill fleece is used for liners, hammocks and other suitable cage accessories.

♡ Dust

Chinchilla dust is needed for regular dust baths and proper coat care.

♡ Apple Wood

Safe apple wood provides natural chewing enrichment and helps support dental health.

♡ Kiln-Dried Pine Wood

Safe kiln-dried pine is used for ledges, platforms, houses and other cage furnishings.

♡ Cages

Suitable cages are essential for providing safe, spacious housing for chinchillas in our care.

♡ Water Bottles & Food Bowls

Safe bottles and bowls are needed for everyday feeding and fresh drinking water.

♡ Dust Houses

Suitable dust houses allow chinchillas to enjoy their regular dust baths safely.

♡ Climate Control

Maintaining a cool and safe environment is especially important for chinchillas because of their sensitivity to heat.

♡ Travel

Travel expenses can be involved when collecting, transporting or helping chinchillas in need.

♡ Emergencies

Unexpected situations can require additional supplies, equipment or immediate care.

♡ Veterinary Care

Donations can help with veterinary examinations, treatment and other medical expenses when needed.
